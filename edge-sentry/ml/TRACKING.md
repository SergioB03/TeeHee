# Sensing and tracking results

Generated by `python3 ml/tracking_eval.py`. Do not edit by hand.

With sensing noise on, each blue sensor detects a contact only some of the time and reports a noisy position. The tracker is a constant-velocity Kalman filter per contact that fuses every sensor's detection, coasts through gaps of up to three ticks, and then drops the track. "No tracker" uses the single best detection of the tick and sees nothing on a tick without one. 100 random rollouts per row.

Position error is the distance between where the picture puts a contact and where it is. Threat AUC and recall are for the default threat model, trained on the perfect picture and not retrained, over the contact-ticks that have a track. Mission value is from rollouts where blue has point defense and a fighter, both run by the autonomy planner from the same picture.


## Does training on noisy tracks help?

The same network retrained on tracker output at mixed noise levels (`python3 ml/train.py --noise-mix`), scored on the same rows. It is an experiment: edge-sim still ships the weights trained on the perfect picture.
