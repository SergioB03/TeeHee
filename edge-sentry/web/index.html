                    Add a unit

                    Rectangles are blue units and diamonds are red. HQ headquarters, SN sensor, RL relay, JM jammer. Aircraft and missiles are drawn as silhouettes pointing the way they are flying, with their altitude level (0 ground to 5) beside them: jet, stealth fighter (F-22 class: fast and hard to see), bomber (slow, heavy, strikes from high up), drone (slow and low) and missile. Attackers must come down to hit a ground target, anything at level 0 or 1 hides behind cover, and point defense needs a lock on a contact to stop it. The 3D view shows aircraft as figures at their altitude, each with a shadow on the ground below it, nose up or down as it climbs or dives, banked while it orbits, and a missile leaves a bright trail; drag sideways to turn the view. Ground units there are small models: a block with a flag is headquarters, a mast with a dish is a sensor, a lattice tower is a relay, and a block with radio waves is a jammer. The shaded circle is a jammer's field, hatched squares are cover, a dotted line is a move order, a red line is an attack, and a dashed blue line is the HQ's point defense covering against a contact (its hit is blocked, or its jam field suppressed). Threat is the trained model's estimate of the chance a contact harms a blue unit within 5 ticks, judged only from how it moves and what it emits. The two marks on each bar are the watch and hostile cut-offs.
